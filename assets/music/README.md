# Music beds

Drop one track here and every render picks it up automatically (first file
wins; override with `--music path/to/track.mp3`).

**Licence rule: CC0 or CC BY only.** Record the credit in the video
description, and keep the source URL in a note next to the file.

Places that are safe if you check the per-track licence:

- Free Music Archive — filter to CC0 / CC BY (https://freemusicarchive.org)
- ccMixter (http://dig.ccmixter.org)
- Wikimedia Commons audio
- Kevin MacLeod / Incompetech — CC BY, attribution required

Avoid anything CC BY-NC, BY-ND or ShareAlike: the first two forbid monetised
use, and the third would pull your whole video under a share-alike licence.

The bed is looped to length, dropped to about 18% under the voice, sidechain-
ducked while the narration is speaking, and faded in over 2 s and out over 3 s.
